# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a welcoming, productive place for contributors and users, regardless of background or experience.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful and constructive. Assume good intent, give actionable technical feedback, respect privacy, and keep discussions relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, threats, discriminatory remarks, deliberate disruption, and publishing someone else's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, lock discussions, or restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer using an established private contact channel. Do not include sensitive personal details in public issues.

Maintainers will review reports in context and take proportionate action. This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed spaces.
