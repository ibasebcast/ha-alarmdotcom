# Alarm.com 2026.10.30b0

Beta build from `beta/2026.10.30`, based on stable 2026.10.28.

## Fixes

Session keep-alive continues after reconnects. Login repair has bounded waits and backoff, preserves authentication errors, and distinguishes a refused endpoint from a rejected login. Polling cannot overwrite a newer live event. Device reconciliation runs after connection gaps and quick open/close events. A device must be absent from three consecutive refreshes before removal, so a temporary incomplete response preserves its entities and customization. Event subscribers are isolated from one another, and deleted devices are cleaned up completely.

Thermostat fan ON and circulate use a duration the device advertises. Indefinite operation is retained when supported; otherwise the shortest supported positive duration is selected. A thermostat with no usable duration raises an explanatory error instead of sending an unsupported command.

## New features

- **Z-Wave scenes:** Scene 001 and Scene 002 event entities, plus device automation triggers with optional KeyPressed, KeyPressed2x through KeyPressed5x, KeyHeldDown, and KeyReleased filters. Supported switches must forward Central Scene notifications through Alarm.com. The `alarmdotcom_scene` bus event includes device_id, resource_id, scene, and value.
- **Light colors:** RGB and white warmth on devices advertising these capabilities. ADC reports white warmth as a percentage; HA's 2700-6500 K slider represents that relative range and is not a calibrated measurement of the bulb. Plain lights and dimmers retain their existing entity identities.
- **Camera analytics:** Person, vehicle, animal, and package binary sensors. A classification stays active for ten seconds after the most recent detection. These require a camera and Alarm.com service plan that supply the relevant analytics events. Cameras without analytics remain idle.
- **Camera playback:** Fullscreen button, including the iPhone video-player fallback. Brief dashboard moves during rotation preserve the stream; actual removal closes it after a one-second grace period. Old connections cannot replace newer streams.

Camera tokens continue to use the authenticated HA WebSocket endpoint and are not exposed as entity attributes. This beta does not replace the integration with HA-ADC's separate domain or library.

## Testing

Automated checks cover session recovery, stale polling, missing-device handling, scene decoding and HA wiring, color commands, classification decoding and pulse timers, and camera-card lifecycle. Live thermostat, color-light, Central Scene switch, camera analytics, and mobile playback verification is still required. Enable beta releases in HACS to install this prerelease, then restart Home Assistant.
